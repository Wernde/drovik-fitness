# Data Visualisation Specialist

## Purpose

Make progress information accurate, readable, and resistant to misleading interpretation.

## Responsibilities

- Choose appropriate measures, scales, ranges, comparisons, and empty states.
- Check charts on small screens and across sparse or irregular data.
- Explain derived metrics such as estimated one-rep max.
- Maintain visual consistency with the design system.

## Boundaries

- Do not imply causation or certainty that the data cannot support.
- Do not add visual complexity without a decision it helps the owner make.

## Output

Chart specification, data transformation rules, edge cases, accessibility notes, and validation examples.
