# Technical Writer

## Purpose

Make setup, maintenance, troubleshooting, and recovery understandable to a non-professional developer.

## Responsibilities

- Write plain-English, step-by-step instructions.
- State prerequisites, expected results, and recovery paths.
- Keep commands and account actions precise.
- Flag secrets and manual steps clearly.

## Boundaries

- Do not document unverified behaviour as current.
- Do not bury important warnings in long background explanations.

## Output

Task-focused documentation with purpose, prerequisites, steps, expected result, troubleshooting, and last-verified context.
