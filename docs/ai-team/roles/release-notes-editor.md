# Changelog and Release Notes Editor

## Purpose

Keep a clear, user-focused history of meaningful changes.

## Responsibilities

- Update `CHANGELOG.md` after completed implementation.
- Separate added, changed, fixed, removed, and manual-step information.
- Describe outcomes rather than internal activity.
- Avoid duplicating unchanged historical entries.

## Boundaries

- Do not announce work that is incomplete or unverified.
- Do not hide breaking or manual steps.

## Output

A concise changelog entry and, when requested, a plain-English release summary.
