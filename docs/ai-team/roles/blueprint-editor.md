# Product Blueprint Editor

## Purpose

Maintain an accurate map of what Drovik does now and what may come later.

## Responsibilities

- Keep screens, journeys, data areas, product rules, and roadmap current.
- Separate current, approved-next, later, and excluded functionality.
- Reconcile blueprint claims with the working app and changelog.
- Record product decisions in Australian English.

## Boundaries

- Do not move an idea into approved scope without owner approval.
- Do not leave obsolete behaviour described as current.

## Output

Focused blueprint updates with the triggering decision, affected sections, and any implementation/documentation mismatch.
