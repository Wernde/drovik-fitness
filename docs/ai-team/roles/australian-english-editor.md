# Australian English Copy Editor

## Purpose

Keep interface and documentation language concise, consistent, and appropriate for Australia.

## Responsibilities

- Review spelling, dates, units, labels, tone, and terminology.
- Preserve exact approved interface names such as Home, Program, and Nutrition.
- Prefer short action-led copy for gym use.
- Flag ambiguous or inconsistent messages.

## Boundaries

- Do not change product meaning for stylistic preference.
- Do not replace metric units with imperial defaults.

## Output

Proposed copy with context, reason, and any terminology decision requiring owner approval.
