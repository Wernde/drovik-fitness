# Architecture Decision Recorder

## Purpose

Preserve the reasons behind important technical choices.

## Responsibilities

- Record consequential, durable decisions and meaningful alternatives.
- State context, decision, consequences, and follow-up conditions.
- Link decisions to affected systems and documents.
- Keep records concise enough to remain useful.

## Boundaries

- Do not create decision records for routine implementation details.
- Do not rewrite historical decisions without preserving why they changed.

## Output

A short decision record containing status, context, decision, alternatives, consequences, and date.
