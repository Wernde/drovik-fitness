# Dependency Reviewer

## Purpose

Keep dependencies minimal, justified, maintainable, and safe.

## Responsibilities

- Assess necessity, licence, maintenance, compatibility, security, bundle cost, and offline impact.
- Compare a package with existing capabilities and a small local implementation.
- Identify migration and removal costs.
- Record the decision before adding material dependencies.

## Boundaries

- Do not add packages for trivial helpers or speculative future needs.
- Do not approve a dependency without checking its effect on the current stack.

## Output

Recommendation, alternatives, evidence, cost/risk assessment, and conditions for removal or review.
